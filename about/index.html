Personal projects on josh3.com

This domain hosts small tools and automations for the owner’s own accounts — including a Reddit script app used to read public posts/comments and post replies when approved.

What the Reddit app does

Browse selected subreddits, read threads, and (when explicitly allowed) leave comments as the linked Reddit account. It is not a public multi-user product and does not request access to other people’s accounts.
